## Cottage Season Means Cleanup Season

If you own a cottage in Grand Bend, Lambton Shores, or anywhere along the Lake Huron shoreline, you know the drill. Every spring you open up to discover a winter's worth of accumulated stuff — and every fall you scramble to clear out before closing up.

Whether you are dealing with old patio furniture that did not survive another Ontario winter, a garage full of broken water toys, or renovations that created a pile of debris, junk has a way of building up at the cottage faster than anywhere else.

Here are our best tips for keeping your cottage property clean and clutter-free.

## 1. Do a Walkthrough Before You Start

Before you start hauling things to the curb, walk through the entire property — inside and out. Make a list of everything that needs to go. Check the garage, shed, under the deck, the dock area, and any storage spaces.

This gives you a clear picture of the scope and helps you decide whether it is a DIY job or something you should call a professional for.

## 2. Sort Into Four Categories

As you go through your items, sort everything into four piles:

- **Keep** — items in good condition that you still use
- **Donate** — usable items that someone else could benefit from
- **Recycle** — materials that can be taken to a recycling facility
- **Dispose** — broken, damaged, or unusable items that need to go

This simple system saves time and ensures you are not sending reusable items to the landfill unnecessarily.

## 3. Know What Your Municipality Accepts

Grand Bend and Lambton Shores have specific rules about what can go to the curb and what needs to be taken to a transfer station. Large items like mattresses, appliances, and construction debris typically cannot be left for regular pickup.

Check with the Municipality of Lambton Shores for current guidelines, or save yourself the hassle and let a junk removal service handle it.

## 4. Plan Around Your Cottage Schedule

If you are opening for the season, tackle the cleanout before you settle in so you can actually enjoy your time at the lake. If you are closing up, schedule your junk removal a week or two before your final visit so you are not rushing.

We offer same-day and next-day service in the Grand Bend area during cottage season, so even last-minute cleanouts are doable.

## 5. Do Not Forget the Outdoor Areas

Cottage junk is not just inside. Common outdoor items that pile up include:

- Broken deck furniture and umbrellas
- Old barbecues and propane tanks
- Kayaks, paddle boards, and water toys past their prime
- Garden waste, branches, and storm debris
- Rotting dock sections or deck boards
- Old sheds or storage structures

We handle all of these, including **shed demolition** and **deck removal** if the structure is beyond repair.

## 6. Watch Out for Hazardous Materials

Cottages often have items stored away that should not go in regular waste — old paint cans, propane tanks, cleaning chemicals, and gasoline containers. These require special disposal and should never be thrown in the trash.

We can advise you on what we can take and what needs to go to a hazardous waste depot.
