## Ad Copy Tips for Meta Ads Manager

- **Primary Text** goes in the main body of the ad (above the image)
- **Headline** appears below the image (bold, ~40 char max ideal)
- **Description** appears below the headline (often truncated on mobile)
- Keep emojis minimal — Ad 10 uses them intentionally for a direct-response feel
- Test 2-3 primary text variations per image to find winners
- Meta's algorithm performs best with 4-6 ads per ad set
